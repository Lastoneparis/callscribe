FAQ

Questions, answered.

How do I record a call?

Put your call on speakerphone, open CallScribe, and tap record. The app captures both sides through your iPhone's microphone, then transcribes and summarizes it on-device.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything happens on your iPhone. Recordings and transcripts never leave the device — there are no cloud uploads, no accounts, and no data collection.

How does the free trial work?

The Weekly and Annual plans include a 3-day free trial. You won't be charged until the trial ends, and you can cancel anytime in your App Store settings. Lifetime is a one-time purchase.

Visit the full Support & FAQ

A quick note on recording: iOS does not allow third-party apps to tap the cellular voice line, so CallScribe records through the microphone — put calls on speakerphone to capture both sides. You are responsible for complying with call-recording and consent laws in your jurisdiction.
